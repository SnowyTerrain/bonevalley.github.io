🏰 Bone Valley v1.0

Fling the skeleton horde off the road before they reach the castle. 50 waves.

Skeletons have no health: they die only when knocked off the road into the water. Pick a tower in the bottom bar, then click a build pad. Click a tower to upgrade, sell or change its target priority. Pick a basic skill at the start (bomb, wind, mine, harpoon or lightning) and cast it on the road with the skill button or B; every 100 kills made with it evolves it into a stronger form. Left-drag rotates the camera, right-drag pans, scroll zooms. Space pauses. On wave 1 and every 3 waves you pick a reward: hex upgrades fill your 6-slot skill tree, items go to your inventory - drag an item onto a tower (2 permanent slots each).

Choose your basic skill

Every 100 kills made with your skill it evolves into its next form (4 forms in total).
